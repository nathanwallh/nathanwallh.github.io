About me

Hi! I am Nathan Wallheimer, a third-year PhD student in Theoretical Computer Science at the Weizmann Institute of Science, advised by Prof. Amir Abboud. My main research area is complexity theory, specifically fine-grained complexity and algorithms. Recently, I have also been working on distributed algorithms and probabilistic extremal combinatorics.

Previously, I completed my Master's at the University of Haifa under the supervision of Prof. Oren Weimann, where we worked on data structures for planar graphs.
